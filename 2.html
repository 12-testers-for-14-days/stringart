Best Practices

Choosing the right image

String art thrives on contrast and clarity. Here's what works best.

Portraits

Faces with strong lighting

Silhouettes

Single clear subjects

Logos

Bold simple shapes

What works well

  • ›High contrast between subject and background
  • ›Minimum 600×600 pixels
  • ›Sharp focus, no motion blur
  • ›Clean or simple backgrounds
  • ›One clear focal subject
  • ›Strong directional lighting

What to avoid

  • ›Busy, cluttered backgrounds
  • ›Low resolution or pixelated images
  • ›Flat, even lighting (no contrast)
  • ›Many small fine details
  • ›Blurry or out-of-focus shots
  • ›Gradients with no clear edges

Pro tips for great results

  • • Crop tightly around your subject before uploading — square crops work best
  • • Use the Threshold adjustment to push mid-tones to pure black/white for cleaner lines
  • • Start with 200 nails and 2500 strings, then refine
  • • Increase Contrast boost to 1.4–1.6 for portraits to deepen shadows
  • • Lower Line opacity if the result looks too dark; raise it if too faint
