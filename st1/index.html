Image guide

      

        
Best
Square-ish portraits, objects, pets, architecture, or logos with a distinct silhouette.

        
Resolution
Aim for at least 1000 px on the short edge. The app downsamples it for analysis.

        
Lighting
Even lighting with clear shadows works better than flat, overexposed photos.

        
Avoid
Strong motion blur, very dark underexposed images, tiny subjects, and cluttered backgrounds.
